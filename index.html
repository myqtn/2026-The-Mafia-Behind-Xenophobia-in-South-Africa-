About the Book

A story
for all
of us.

This is a simple book written in a child-like voice so that anyone can understand it. But inside its simple words lives a very big truth about power, money, and what happens when ordinary people are turned against each other.

Through the eyes of Senzo — a small boy who does not exist in the system — we see the machinery of xenophobia not as random hatred, but as a carefully engineered tool. Someone profits when neighbours fight. Someone drives away in a shiny black car while the streets burn.

This book is for South Africa. But its truth belongs to every country where people have been made to blame each other instead of looking up at the hands that hold the strings.
